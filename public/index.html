Why Midway

Built for the way people store things out here

  • Pull straight inEvery unit is drive-up. Back the trailer to the door, or drive the side-by-side right inside.
  • Tall, wide unitsThe M, L and XL units are 14 feet wide and 14 to 15 feet tall, with a red line painted on the concrete so you back in straight the first time.
  • Come and go on your scheduleOpen 24/7 with no gate and no code. Hook up at 5 a.m. for opening day or drop off after a late shift.
  • Everything onlineRent, sign in, pay and set up automatic payments from your phone. No trip to an office.
Bigger than a unit

Shop space, a heated shop and outdoor parking

Need room to work, not just to store? Midway also rents a 12 x 40 shop, a 42 x 42 heated shop and a 36 x 45 shop with 16-foot ceilings. Outdoor 8 x 25 spaces hold trailers and work rigs.

Shops fill up and usually run on a waitlist, so get your name on the list early.
