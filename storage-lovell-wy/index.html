Hometown storage

Storage units in Lovell, Wyoming

Midway Storage sits on the edge of town, a few minutes from Main Street. Store the overflow from the house without driving to another town.

What Lovell renters keep here

For a lot of Lovell households, storage just means clearing room at home. Furniture between moves, the holiday boxes, the garage that stopped fitting a car years ago. The A and C units, 9 by 15 and 8 by 15, handle that without paying for space you won't use.

Being this close changes how people use a unit. You can run over on a lunch break for the camp chairs, or drop off a load after work and still be home for supper. With no gate and no code, a quick trip really is quick.

When the stuff gets bigger, the M units give you 14 feet of height for the camper or the ranch truck with a rack.
